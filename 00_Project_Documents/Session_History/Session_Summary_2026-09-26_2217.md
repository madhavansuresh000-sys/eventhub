## EventHub in one line
From poster to gate: **Plan → Approve → Publish → Book (10-min seat hold) → Waitlist → QR check-in → Certificate**

## Signature features
1. A booking engine that never oversells: a 10-minute seat hold plus optimistic locking
2. A smart waitlist that offers freed seats automatically
3. QR e-tickets that volunteers scan on their phones at the gate
4. PDF certificates, only for students who attended, each with a verify link
5. An approval workflow and an organizer analytics dashboard
